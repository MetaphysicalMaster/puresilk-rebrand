Our Story

The House We're Tending

Pure Silk Beauty has been a fixture in Upper Arlington for years — a small house of care where women came to be seen.

We took stewardship of that house with a single promise: to honor what was already loved, and to deepen what could be deepened.

The taglines remained. The team grew. The standard rose.

The covenant — that every woman who walks through the door leaves more herself than when she came — that's the part we hold like silk.

This is a chapter. You're invited in.
